How to play 🧧🎄

In 30 seconds

  1. Tap Play.
  2. Drag your finger left and right (or use the ← → keys) to move the CakeTella tiramisu.
  3. Catch the falling ang pao (presents at Christmas) and cake slices on the tiramisu.
  4. Dodge the firecrackers (or lumps of coal). Each one takes 3 points away.
  5. When time is up, you get your reward code on screen.

Points

Ang pao / present / cake slice+1
Golden "Cake of the Month" slice+3
Firecracker / coal−3

Rewards

10+ pointsRed ang pao: RM5 off any cake
20+ pointsSilver ang pao: RM10 off a Tiramisu Kahlua
30+ pointsGold ang pao: free Martell Raisin Ice Cream Tub with any whole cake

Tap Order on WhatsApp on the win screen to send your code straight to CakeTella, or show it at the PJ shop (35, Jalan SS 26/15, Taman Mayang Jaya). One reward per order. 18+ only.

Tips

  • The golden slice is worth chasing: three of them is almost a whole tier.
  • Things fall faster near the end, so stay in the middle.
  • Tap 🔊 Sound at the top to mute or unmute.
  • You can play again as many times as you like.
